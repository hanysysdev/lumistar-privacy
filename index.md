Privacy Policy for Lumistar
Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Lumistar ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our Android mobile application Lumistar (the "App").

1. Information Processed Locally
To function as an IPTV media player, Lumistar processes the following information strictly on your local device:

IPTV Credentials & Playlists: IPTV M3U/M3U8 URLs, Xtream Codes API credentials (server URL, username, password), or playlist configurations provided directly by you.

App Preferences & Favorites: Favorite channels, movies, series, watch history, and custom playlist configurations.

Zero Data Transmission to Us: All playlist URLs, Xtream credentials, favorites, and settings remain stored exclusively within the App’s local storage on your device. We do not operate a backend server to collect, record, host, or transmit your credentials, playlist links, or viewing habits.

2. Connection to Third-Party IPTV Services
When you input your IPTV URL or Xtream Codes credentials, the App connects directly from your device to your chosen IPTV service provider to retrieve channel lists and stream media content.

We do not host, sell, manage, or supply any IPTV playlists or content streams.

Any data sent during streaming (such as requests to play a stream) occurs directly between your Android device and your IPTV service provider. Please refer to your IPTV provider's terms and privacy policy regarding their server practices.

3. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising
No Analytics: Lumistar does not use tracking tools, diagnostic telemetry, or analytics SDKs to track app usage.

No Accounts: Lumistar does not require registration, login accounts, or personal details (such as your name or email address).

No Advertisements: The App does not contain third-party ad networks or ad-tracking tools.

4. Device Permissions
Lumistar may request standard permissions to deliver media player functionality:

Internet Access: Required solely to load playlist data and stream content from your designated IPTV provider directly to your device.

Storage / Media Access: Used locally to save offline playlist caches, app preferences, or local configurations on your device.

5. Data Deletion and Storage Management
Because all playlist configurations, credentials, and favorite items are stored strictly in the App's local storage:

You can instantly clear all stored data, playlists, and credentials at any time by clearing the App's data via Android Settings > Apps > Lumistar > Storage > Clear Data.

Uninstalling Lumistar permanently removes all saved playlists, credentials, and favorites from your device.

6. Children's Privacy
Because Lumistar does not collect, request, or share any personal information, it fully complies with international children's privacy regulations, including COPPA and GDPR.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
